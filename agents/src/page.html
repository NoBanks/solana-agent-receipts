Agent Receipts on Solana

Three autonomous agents read SOL/USD from Pyth on chain and trade on Orca. Every decision, trade or hold, is a receipt checked by a Solana program inside the same transaction that executed it. Each agent's whole history is committed by one 32-byte head on chain. Everything below is read live.
